Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 27, 2026

1. Introduction

CryptoAI Assistant ("we", "our", or "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our educational cryptocurrency content generation service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide the Service:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • TikTok OAuth Tokens: When you connect your TikTok account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (user ID) required for the Content Posting API
  • Content Preferences: Educational topics and content settings you select

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the Service (features used, content generated)
  • Technical Information: Browser type, device information, IP address (for security purposes)

2.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Financial information or investment data
  • Personal identification documents
  • Bank account or cryptocurrency wallet information
  • Sensitive personal data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for providing and improving the Service:

  • To generate educational cryptocurrency content
  • To upload content to your TikTok account via the Content Posting API
  • To improve the quality of educational content generation
  • To ensure compliance with TikTok's API terms
  • To maintain Service security and prevent abuse

4. TikTok API Integration

When you connect your TikTok account:

  • We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication
  • We request minimal permissions: video.upload and video.publish only
  • We do not access your TikTok friends list, messages, or private content
  • We only upload educational content that you approve
  • You can revoke access at any time through TikTok's settings

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Limited sharing occurs only when necessary:

  • TikTok: We share educational content with TikTok's servers via the Content Posting API
  • Service Providers: We may use trusted third parties for hosting and maintenance (e.g., cloud services)
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights

6. Data Security

Our Security Principles:

  • All API tokens are encrypted and securely stored
  • We use industry-standard security practices
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Access to personal data is strictly limited to necessary personnel

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary:

  • TikTok Tokens: Retained while your account is connected, deleted immediately upon disconnection
  • Usage Data: Retained for 30 days for analytics and improvement
  • Content Preferences: Retained to personalize your experience

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your data:

  • Access: You can request information about your data
  • Correction: You can update your preferences
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your data
  • TikTok Access: You can revoke TikTok access at any time
  • Opt-Out: You can stop using the Service at any time

9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.
